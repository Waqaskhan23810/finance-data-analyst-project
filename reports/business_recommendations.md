# Management Recommendations Framework

1. Review revenue-product and revenue-region concentration before changing commercial targets.
2. Investigate departments with persistent unfavorable expense variance, distinguishing price, volume, and timing effects.
3. Prioritize overdue receivables and monitor liquidity ratios alongside cash-flow forecasts.
4. Review statistically unusual transactions with source documentation; anomaly flags are review prompts, not allegations.
5. Reforecast cash and operating plans when revenue growth or margin trends deviate materially from budget.
