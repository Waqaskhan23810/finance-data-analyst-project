# Executive Summary

Meridian Industrial Systems is a fictional B2B manufacturer. All data is synthetic and exists solely to demonstrate a reproducible finance analytics workflow.

The dashboard and notebooks calculate revenue, cost of goods sold, operating expenses, gross/operating/net profit, cash flows, liquidity, leverage, budget variance, and working-capital indicators directly from the generated processed data. Management should use the reports to investigate trends and material variances; no inference in this project is a claim about a real company.
